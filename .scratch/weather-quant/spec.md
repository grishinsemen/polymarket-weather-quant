# Polymarket Weather Quant Engine & Pro Terminal

Status: resolved

## Problem Statement

Retail participants in Polymarket daily temperature markets routinely price outcomes from generic city-wide weather apps, missing the fact that contracts settle against specific ICAO airport stations, enforce strict `Routine METAR` versus `SPECI` qualification rules, and charge `weather_fees` to spread-crossing takers while `Maker Order` quotes trade at zero fee. Operating a small `$100` bankroll manually across global timezones is impractical when fusing ~120-member weather ensembles, live aviation sensor updates, and orderbook movements—and without strict version attribution and deterministic replay over archived market snapshots, it is impossible to know whether a model tweak or rule change genuinely improved `Edge` and `Closing Line Value (CLV)`.

## Solution

An autonomous 24/7 weather quant engine, reproducible experimentation lab, low-noise Telegram radar, and dark pro web terminal built for a `$100` bankroll on Polymarket daily ICAO `METAR` temperature markets. The system discovers eligible `Weather Event` markets, estimates calibrated `Fair Probability` distributions across every `Bucket` using a 3-layer `Ensemble Distribution` + `Station Bias` + live `Routine METAR` core augmented by leak-free Quantile ML MOS on top-volume `Station` markets, executes a two-phase `Maker Order` lifecycle (`Premarket Entry` and `Intraday Repricing`) via a realistic 24/7 `PaperExecutor` within strict `City Exposure` and `Intraday Cash Reserve` limits, archives all live streams to columnar storage, tags every prediction, order, fill, and simulation with `Model Version` and `Strategy Version`, and runs deterministic `Experiment Replay` comparisons before promoting updates.

## Implementation Decisions

- **Station Registry & Rule Parser Module**: Deep module mapping Polymarket `Weather Event` metadata to normalized domain objects (`Station` ICAO identifier, runway coordinates/elevation, local day window, polarity, `Bucket` intervals, `Remark Tenths` flag, `weather_fees` parameters) and disqualifying non-`METAR` or ambiguous markets.
- **METAR Observation Parser Module**: Pure deterministic parser normalizing raw aviation report strings, rejecting `SPECI` headers, extracting observation timestamps, wind/cloud indicators, integer temperatures, and `RMK` `T`-group `Remark Tenths` values.
- **Hybrid Probability & MOS Module**: Exposes a narrow boundary `(Station, WeatherEvent, EnsembleSnapshot, RecentBiasWindow, IntradayMetarSeries, ModelVersion) -> BucketFairProbabilities`. Combines the 3-layer baseline (`ECMWF + GFS + ICON` ~120 trajectories, 30–45 day rolling `Station Bias`, intraday `Routine METAR` cutoff/trend conditioning) with out-of-band trained Quantile GBDT (`LightGBM` / `CatBoost`) MOS quantile adjustments on top `Station` markets.
- **Version Registry & Columnar Replay Archive Module**: Relational database schema defines immutable `model_versions` and `strategy_versions` tables (capturing parameter/feature hashes, training windows, and calibration scores). Operational tables (`predictions`, `signals`, `orders`, `fills`, `positions`, `clv_snapshots`, `experiment_runs`) store foreign keys to both `model_version` and `strategy_version`. Live `CLOB` snapshots, `Ensemble Distribution` pulls, and `Routine METAR` observations append to partitioned `Parquet` archives with `ingested_at_utc` stamps. An `Experiment Replay` runner drives the exact same pricing and strategy engine over archived `Parquet` feeds using a virtual `Clock`, writing tagged `experiment_runs` summaries for side-by-side comparison.
- **Two-Phase Strategy, Risk & Paper Execution Module**: Pure decision engine evaluating `Fair Probability` against live `CLOB` top-5 depth under active `Strategy Version` constraints (`$100` reference bankroll, `0.2x` Fractional Kelly, `$5` per `Bucket`, `$8` `City Exposure`, `40%` `Intraday Cash Reserve`, `Edge >= 5¢`, stale-feed and liquidity guards). Order placement crosses a narrow `OrderExecutor` seam (`PaperExecutor` tracking resting `Post-Only` `Maker Order` quotes over live `CLOB` WebSocket streams with 10-second `PING` keepalive, filling only on opposing touch or trade-through, computing `weather_fees` on any taker exit, and logging post-entry `CLV`).
- **Backend Gateway & Telegram Radar Module**: Async `FastAPI` service running `asyncio` workers (`httpx`, `websockets`, `aiosqlite`), offloading heavy ML training and `Experiment Replay` batch jobs outside the live orderbook event loop, serving typed REST endpoints (screener, station X-ray, portfolio/orders, version registry, `Experiment Replay` trigger/comparison, pause/resume controls) and a live WebSocket stream, plus an async Telegram notifier with per-transition deduplication.
- **Pro Web Terminal Module**: Standalone `Next.js` + `React` dark terminal consuming the typed backend REST/WebSocket contract to render the `Model Version` / `Strategy Version` filtered portfolio header, multi-city `Edge` Screener, 11-`Bucket` City X-Ray (orderbook depth vs `Fair Probability`, `Ensemble Distribution` fan + live `Routine METAR` trajectory, order log), and Experiment Replay comparison view.

## Testing Decisions

- **Testing Philosophy**: Verify observable behavior and invariants across high seams using deterministic fixtures rather than mocking internal formulas, database queries, or helper methods.
- **Primary Engine & API Seam**: Behavioral tests feed deterministic `World / Feed` snapshots and controllable `Clock` ticks into the engine/API and assert end-to-end outcomes: `Fair Probability` distribution normalization (`~1.00` across a `Weather Event`), `SPECI` rejection, `Remark Tenths` accuracy, `Maker Order` placement and early exit behavior, `$5`/`$8`/`40%` risk caps, touch/trade-through fill mechanics, `CLV` computation, and accurate `model_version` / `strategy_version` persistence in `SQLite`.
- **Temporal Integrity & Replay Seam**: Automated tests on the Historical Backtest and `Parquet` `Experiment Replay` adapters enforce strict `timestamp < decision_time` guards (failing on any future leak) and prove bit-for-bit determinism across repeated `Experiment Replay` runs for identical `(Model Version, Strategy Version, dataset)` inputs.
- **Frontend Terminal Seam**: Component/integration tests pass deterministic REST/WebSocket state fixtures into the `Next.js` terminal views to verify Screener sorting, 11-`Bucket` X-Ray visualization, and version-filtered `CLV`/PnL and replay comparison tables.

## Out of Scope

- Non-`METAR` weather settlement authorities in V1 (such as Hong Kong Observatory or global monthly anomaly indices).
- Any circumvention of Polymarket geographic access controls, proxy spoofing, or undocumented APIs.
- Fee-paying taker sniping strategies across spreads.
- Non-weather Polymarket categories (Tennis, Crypto, Macroeconomics) and Bybit V5 strategies in V1 (planned for future separate specs).
